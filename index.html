02 Design principles

Principles youcan touch.

Four rules I design by. Each card below is a small working demo, so try them instead of just reading about them.

P.01 / PerceptionWCAG 1.4.3

Contrast is kindness.

Low-contrast text is hard to read for people with low vision, on cheap screens, or outside in bright sun. Drag the slider and watch the text pass or fail.

Apply for the scholarshipTakes about 3 minutes. You can save and finish later.
4.5:1AAAAA
P.02 / MotorFitts's Law

Big enough for real thumbs.

Small tap targets cause mis-taps, especially for older users and people with tremors. I keep touch targets at 44px or larger.

44pxComfortable
P.03 / TrustSystem status

Never leave people guessing.

On a slow connection, a button that gives no feedback gets tapped again and again. Every action should clearly say it's working, then say it's done.

IdleWorking…Done
P.04 / CognitionHick's Law

Fewer choices, faster yes.

Every extra option adds time to a decision. I show the one thing people need right now and keep the rest a tap away.
